Version 0.1.2

- Increased the vanilla Wood Bow base ATK from 2 to 3.
- Added the Reforge 1 Splash Damage outcome: 1.5-tile radius and 50% direct damage.
- Added the Reforge 2 Bleed outcome: three one-second ticks at 3% maximum health for normal targets and 1% for EnemyCategory.Boss.
- Added Bleed application and refresh from successful splash hits.
- Added support for hostile GameUnit, AnimalBody, MapObj and vanilla bow-damageable EnemyNexus targets.
- Excluded friendly GameUnit targets and ordinary friendly buildings from splash selection.
- Made percentage damage use nearest-integer rounding so actual health loss and floating damage text match.
- Changed the in-game Bleed tooltip to display only 流血.
- Added scene-change and shutdown cleanup for active Bleed states.
